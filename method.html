Method

Instruments

Two Pimoroni Enviro+ boards with PMS5003 particulate sensors, outdoors in Abertridwr: one in a rear garden, one at roof level. PM1, PM2.5 and PM10 are recorded every two seconds and stored as five-minute means, plus a one-minute series with wind since 2026-11-17. Wind comes from the nearest OpenWeather station and is indicative — the valley has its own local flow.

What the data can and cannot show

These are low-cost optical sensors, not reference monitors. They cannot identify a source, cannot distinguish wood smoke from other combustion, and are affected by humidity. An episode is only counted when both units rise together; a single-sensor spike is a local artefact.

Against the WHO guidelines

The History page sets daily means against the WHO global air quality guidelines for PM2.5 (2021): 15 µg/m³ as a 24-hour mean and 5 µg/m³ as an annual mean. Two cautions apply. WHO describes these as reference values rather than safe limits — the evidence shows health effects continuing below them, and the guidelines were set at the lowest levels at which harm is demonstrable, not at a threshold. And the sensors' own accuracy, roughly ±10 µg/m³, is as wide as the entire gap between the two lines, so a reading near either value cannot on its own prove which side of it the true concentration fell. The comparison is published because it is the standard everyone else measures against, not because this equipment can resolve it finely.

Known limitations

  • The garden unit's BME280 humidity channel is faulty (railed at 0xFFFF) and is excluded; only the roof humidity is published.
  • Gas and noise channels are recorded locally but not published — uncalibrated.
  • The roof unit may read drier and catch plumes the garden unit misses.
  • The archive before 2026-11-17 is five-minute snapshots only.

Why this exists

To give Caerphilly County Borough Council and residents a local, indicative record of evening PM2.5 in a valley with no reference monitor, and to support a request for proper monitoring rather than to accuse anyone.
