WHO global air quality guidelines (2021) for PM2.5: 15 µg/m³ as a 24-hour mean, set as a 99th percentile — about three or four days a year may exceed it — and 5 µg/m³ as an annual mean. This record covers 2025-10-28 to 2026-11-26, so the mean over the period is indicative next to an annual guideline rather than a like-for-like year, and the sensors' own accuracy of roughly ±10 µg/m³ is as wide as the gap between the two lines. WHO states that no threshold has been found below which exposure is harmless, so these are reference lines, not safe limits.
